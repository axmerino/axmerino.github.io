How I think

The approach.

Good engineering is less about the specific tools and more about the thinking behind the tradeoffs.

Simplicity over cleverness

The best systems are the ones your future self can still read at 2am.

Observability first

You can't fix what you can't see. Logging, tracing, and metrics aren't extras — they're load-bearing.

AI as a collaborator

Not a magic layer on top. Thoughtful integration means knowing when not to reach for it.

Ship, then refine

Momentum beats perfection. Working software tells you things your whiteboard never will.
